Our Story & Origins

From 2 MW in Rajasthan to a national clean energy force.

Sor Connect was established in 2020 by veteran renewable energy professionals driven by a single mission: to make world-class, custom-engineered solar energy systems dependable, affordable, and accessible for businesses, farms, and households across India.

What began as a dedicated solar plant maintenance project managing just 2 MW in Rajasthan has rapidly expanded into a recognized industry leader. Today, we oversee 150+ MW across 13 Indian states with an in-house force of 130+ engineers, technicians, and project managers.

From industrial rooftop EPC and agricultural solarisation under PM-KUSUM to residential installations under PM Surya Ghar and 25-year predictive O&M, we deliver end-to-end solar solutions with zero compromise on safety, yield, or quality.
