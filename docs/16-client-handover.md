# 16. Client Owner & Executive Management Manual

## 1. Welcome to Your Web Platform

Congratulations on the launch of your new corporate web platform for **Sor Connect** ([www.sorconnect.in](https://www.sorconnect.in)).

This guide is written in **plain, non-technical language** specifically for the business owner, managing director, marketing managers, and HR administrators. It explains how to manage your website on a day-to-day basis without needing a computer programmer.

---

## 2. Accessing Your Private Management Portal

You have a private management control panel that allows you to manage customer inquiries, job applications, portfolio projects, and media.

1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari).
2. Type in your private address:  
   👉 **`https://www.sorconnect.in/admprtl`**
3. Enter your administrator username and password provided in your handover envelope.
4. Click **Sign In**.

---

## 3. Daily Business Tasks You Can Do Yourself

---

### Task 1: Check New Customer Inquiries (Leads)
When an industrial plant manager or homeowner fills out a form on your site:
1. Log in to the Admin Portal.
2. Click **Form Submissions** in the left sidebar.
3. You will see the customer's:
   - **Name**
   - **WhatsApp / Mobile Number** (Click to immediately start a WhatsApp chat or phone call)
   - **Monthly Electricity Bill** (e.g. ₹50,000+)
   - **Pincode & Location**
   - **Their Message / Requirements**
4. Change the status from `New` to `Contacted` once your sales team speaks with them.

---

### Task 2: Review Job Applications & Download Resumes
When engineers apply through the careers portal:
1. Click **Job Applications** in the left sidebar.
2. Review the candidate's years of experience, position applied for, and availability.
3. Click **Download CV** to view their attached PDF resume.
4. Change the candidate's status to `Interview Scheduled` or `Selected`.

---

### Task 3: Add a New Completed Solar Project
Whenever your engineering team finishes a new solar installation:
1. Click **Projects Portfolio** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the green **`+ Add Project`** button at the top right.
3. Fill in:
   - **Project Title:** (e.g. `1.2 MW Industrial Ground Mount`)
   - **Client Name:** (e.g. `ABC Steel & Rolling Mills`)
   - **Capacity:** (e.g. `1200 kW`)
   - **Location:** (e.g. `Sitapura Industrial Area, Jaipur`)
   - **Category:** Select `Commercial Rooftop`, `Turnkey EPC`, or `O&M`
   - **Image:** Click **Upload Image** to select a photo from your computer.
4. Click **Save Project**.  
   *It will appear immediately on your live website portfolio!*

---

### Task 4: Add New Photos to the About Us Slideshow
To show off your latest field installations on the About Us page:
1. Click **About Us Carousel** under the **Media & Proof** section in the left sidebar.
2. Click **`+ Add Carousel Slide`**.
3. Enter the plant title, location, capacity, and upload a high-quality landscape photo.
4. Click **Save Slide**.

## 4. What You Can Safely Change vs. What Requires a Developer

| Website Section | Can You Change It Yourself? | How to Do It |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer Inquiries** | ✅ YES | In Admin Portal &rarr; Form Submissions |
| **Job Applications** | ✅ YES | In Admin Portal &rarr; Job Applications |
| **Project Photos & Case Studies** | ✅ YES | In Admin Portal &rarr; Projects Portfolio |
| **About Us Photos & Carousel** | ✅ YES | In Admin Portal &rarr; About Us Carousel |
| **Awards & Certifications** | ✅ YES | In Admin Portal &rarr; Awards & Recognitions |
| **Partner Logos** | ✅ YES | In Admin Portal &rarr; Partners & Alliances |
| **Office Phone Numbers & Email** | ⚠️ Needs Developer | Hardcoded in HTML headers and footers |
| **Company Address on Contact Page** | ⚠️ Needs Developer | Hardcoded in HTML and Google Schema files |
| **Page Layouts & Color Themes** | ❌ NEVER Touch | Requires Frontend Engineer (CSS/HTML) |
| **Domain & DNS Settings** | ❌ NEVER Touch | Requires DevOps / SysAdmin |

## 5. Critical Safety Warnings for Business Owners

> [!CAUTION]
> 1. **Never Share Admin Passwords Publicly:** Keep your admin portal login credentials secure within your trusted management team.
> 2. **Deleting Data is Permanent:** When you click the red Trash Can icon to delete a project or customer lead, it is permanently deleted from the database.
> 3. **Never Allow Unverified Third Parties to Modify DNS:** Your domain DNS records connect your website to the server. Altering DNS can take your website completely offline.
